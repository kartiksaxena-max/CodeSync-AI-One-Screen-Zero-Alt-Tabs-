# Evidence for the problem

Ask 5 to 10 people who learn or teach coding. Keep it to 2 minutes each. Use their real answers only.

## Questions

1. When you follow a coding video, slides or a book, how many separate windows do you use?
2. In a 30-minute lesson, about how many times do you switch windows?
3. What do you lose when you switch (your place in the video, your code, your train of thought)?
4. How often do you rewind to find where the teacher wrote something?
5. Would having the video, slides and editor in one window help? Why or why not?
